Rules
  • Strictly a league competition: no final and no third-place match.
  • Each team plays the other two twice, home and away: 4 matches per team, 6 in total.
  • Fixture order: Team A v Team B, Team C v Team A, Team B v Team C. Each team plays 2 matches per matchday.
  • Points: Win 3 · Draw 1 · Loss 0.
  • Tie-breakers when level on points: 1) goal difference, 2) head-to-head record.
  • The team top of the table at the end is crowned PRIME FC Mini 4-a-side League Champion.
